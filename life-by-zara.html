Every piece starts on someone's finger,
not a spreadsheet.

Zara trained as a silversmith in Florence before opening a one-room studio in Södermalm. Ten years on, the workshop still turns away more commissions than it takes — each piece is filed, soldered and set by hand, and no design leaves the bench until it's been worn for a week first.

Life by Zara isn't a catalogue. It's a running record of the rings people asked for when something in their life changed — an engagement, a first paycheque, a name they wanted to keep close.

— Zara
